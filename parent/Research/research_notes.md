# Parent Wellness Research Notes

## Core Challenges for Modern Parents
- **Time Poverty:** Juggle career responsibilities with childcare and house management.
- **Mental Load:** Continuously organizing schedules, meals, doctor appointments, and educational milestones.
- **Parental Burnout:** Feeling emotionally exhausted, detached from children, and ineffective as a caregiver.
- **Isolation:** Lack of structural community support.

## Wellness Objectives
1. **Reduce Daily Friction:** Provide simple micro-reflection templates and focus tasks.
2. **Promote Self-Care:** Emphasize that parental wellbeing is a prerequisite to family health ("you cannot pour from an empty cup").
3. **Enhance Family Communication:** Provide tools for resolving family conflicts peacefully and checking in on family time.
4. **Accessible Calming Routines:** Rapid breathing exercises, meditation triggers, and quick access to parenting insights.
