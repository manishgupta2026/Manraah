# Mindful Parenting Journey Documentation

Welcome to the documentation for the Mindful Parenting Category. This category is dedicated to providing supportive tools and spaces for parents to manage their personal wellness while maintaining healthy relationship dynamics with their family.

## Brand Principles
- **Compassion First:** Avoid parenting advice that sounds judgmental or demanding. Every communication should feel warm, empathetic, and calming.
- **Micro-steps:** Make exercises short (2-5 minutes) since parents have extremely limited time.
- **Holistic Care:** Support the parent as an individual (Personal Care) as well as within their role as a caregiver (Family Wellness).

## Target Audience Persona
- **Name:** Elena (38 years old)
- **Status:** Parent of two (ages 5 and 8), working part-time.
- **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed during morning routines and bedtimes, constantly feeling behind on chores, lack of time for personal reflection, high emotional reactivity when tired.
- **Goals:** Find moments of deep breathing, track daily sleep and water intake, receive non-judgmental guidance, and keep family communication calm and healthy.
