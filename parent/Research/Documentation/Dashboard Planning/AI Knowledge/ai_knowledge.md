# AI Knowledge Base - Mindful Parenting

This file acts as a knowledge source for prompt guidelines and algorithms supporting the Parenting AI Companion.

## Calming Prompt Guidelines
When the parent triggers the "I'm Feeling Overwhelmed" support, guide them through a sensory 5-4-3-2-1 grounding check:
- **5 things you can see:** Look around and identify five visual items.
- **4 things you can feel:** Notice physical contact (feet on the ground, clothes on shoulders).
- **3 things you can hear:** Listen for background sounds (birds, distant traffic, air conditioner).
- **2 things you can smell:** Find two aromas in the immediate space.
- **1 thing you can taste:** Notice the current taste in your mouth.

## Parenting Scenarios & Responses
- **Scenario: Toddler Tantrums**
  - *Advice:* "A child's meltdown is a reflection of their nervous system being overloaded, not your parenting. Drop your shoulders, breathe in deeply for 4 seconds, and meet their big feelings with your calm."
- **Scenario: Sleep Struggles**
  - *Advice:* "Establish a warm, slow wind-down routine 1 hour before bed. Eliminate screens, dim lights, and read a gentle story together. Keep your own energy calm and grounded as they transition to sleep."
- **Scenario: Screen Time Battles**
  - *Advice:* "Establish clear, collaborative boundaries in advance rather than in the heat of the moment. Use a visual timer and offer a pleasant transition activity (like a shared puzzle or outdoor play) to ease the shift."
